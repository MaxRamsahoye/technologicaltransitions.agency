Sources

Works drawn on in A Critical Evolutionary Game-Theory Model of Major Technological Transitions.

  1. Allan Dafoe

    On Technological Determinism: A Typology, Scope Conditions, and a Mechanism

    Science, Technology, & Human Values, 2015

    The mechanism of military-economic adaptationism: societies adopt the technologies that confer military and economic advantage, or are outcompeted by those that do.

  2. Daniel Schmachtenberger

    The Emergence Model

    A society as three interacting layers – infrastructure, social structure and superstructure – through which a new technology stack reshapes political economy and cultural psychology.

    • Derived from

    • Ken Wilber

      Integral Theory

      The interior and exterior, individual and collective dimensions of any whole.

    • Marvin Harris

      Cultural Materialism: The Struggle for a Science of Culture

      1979

      Infrastructure, structure and superstructure, with material conditions as the primary driver of cultural change.

  3. Daniel Schmachtenberger

    New Economics, Part IV

    The historical emergence of the economy as something separate from ecology.

  4. Peter Joseph

    The New Human Rights Movement: Reinventing the Economy to End Oppression

    2017

    The root socio-economic orientation from which structural inequality and oppression follow.

  5. Robert Sapolsky

    Behave: The Biology of Humans at Our Best and Worst

    2017

    The invention of poverty: how agriculture, surplus and hierarchy introduced inequality on a scale unknown to hunter-gatherers.

  6. Jared Diamond

    The Worst Mistake in the History of the Human Race

    Discover, 1987

    Guns, Germs, and Steel: The Fates of Human Societies

    1997

    Agriculture as a mixed blessing – bringing class, inequality and disease – and geography and domestication as the shapers of which societies came to dominate.

  7. Marshall Sahlins

    The Original Affluent Society

    In Stone Age Economics, 1972

    Hunter-gatherers as affluent in a minimalist sense: few wants, readily met.

  8. Luke Kemp

    Goliath’s Curse: The History and Future of Societal Collapse

    2025

    The rise and collapse of dominance hierarchies across history, and what they mean for the future of civilisation.
