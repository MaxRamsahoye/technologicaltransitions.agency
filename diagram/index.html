The Civilisational Tech Stack

The major technological transitions discussed in A Multi-Agent Model of Major Technological Transitions.

Every civilisation stands on a stack of technologies laid down by the revolutions before it. Each new layer – from abstract thought to artificial intelligence – gave the groups that adopted it a decisive advantage, spread across civilisation through competitive pressure, and reshaped the political economy and cultural psychology built upon it. The stack is cumulative: no layer is ever removed, and the newest is being laid now.
